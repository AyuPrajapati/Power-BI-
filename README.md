# 📊 Power BI Dashboard Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates the use of **Microsoft Power BI** for analyzing and visualizing sales data. The main objective of this project is to transform raw data into meaningful and interactive visualizations that help users understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, revenue generation, and store-wise comparisons.

The dashboard provides a simple and effective way to analyze large amounts of data using charts, tables, and graphical representations. Instead of manually reading raw datasets, users can quickly identify important trends and patterns through data visualization.

---

# 🎯 Objectives of the Project

The main objectives of this Power BI project are:

- To analyze customer-wise sales performance.
- To identify products with the highest number of units sold.
- To analyze daily and weekly sales patterns.
- To calculate revenue generated by different products.
- To compare product prices across multiple stores.
- To represent data using interactive charts and tables.
- To convert raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

---

# 🛠️ Tools and Technologies Used

The following tools and technologies were used in this project:

- **Microsoft Power BI** – For creating interactive dashboards and visualizations.
- **Microsoft Excel** – For storing and managing the dataset.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – For creating calculated columns and measures.
- **Data Visualization Techniques** – For representing data through charts, tables, and graphs.

# 📊 Dashboard 1 – Sales Performance Analysis

![Dashboard 1](images/dashboard1.png)

## Customer-Wise Sales Analysis

This section of the dashboard displays the number of sales associated with different customers.

The customer-wise analysis helps in identifying which customers are responsible for a larger number of sales transactions. This information can be useful for understanding customer behavior and identifying important customers.

---

## Product-Wise Sales Analysis

The product table displays the total number of units sold for different products.

By comparing the sales quantity of different products, it becomes easier to identify:

- High-demand products.
- Low-performing products.
- Products with strong customer demand.
- Products that may require additional marketing or promotional strategies.

---

## Daily Sales Analysis

The **Sum of Units Sold by Day** bar chart represents the sales quantity for different days.

This visualization helps identify variations in daily sales performance. Some days may have significantly higher sales, while others may show lower demand.

Daily analysis is useful for understanding short-term sales patterns and identifying periods of high customer activity.

---

## Weekly Sales Analysis

The **Sum of Units Sold by Day of Week** chart represents sales performance across different days of the week.

This analysis helps determine which days generate higher sales. Businesses can use this information for better planning, inventory management, and promotional activities.

For example, if a particular day consistently has higher sales, additional inventory can be made available to meet customer demand.

# 💰 Dashboard 2 – Product and Revenue Analysis

![Dashboard 2](images/dashboard2.png)

## Product Price Analysis

This dashboard displays the price of different products along with the total number of units sold.

Price analysis is important because product price can directly influence customer demand and sales performance.

The dashboard makes it possible to compare:

- Product Name
- Product Price
- Total Units Sold

This comparison helps users understand the relationship between product pricing and sales quantity.

---

## Units Sold Analysis

The **Sum of Units Sold** column represents the total quantity of each product sold.

Products with higher units sold can be considered high-demand products. Products with lower sales may require further analysis to determine whether their lower performance is caused by price, customer demand, availability, or other factors.

---

## Revenue Analysis

The revenue visualization shows the total revenue generated by each product.

Revenue is calculated using the following formula:

### Revenue = Price × Units Sold

Revenue analysis helps identify products that generate the highest financial value for the business.

A product may have a high selling price but low units sold, while another product may have a lower price but significantly higher sales. Revenue analysis helps compare the overall financial contribution of each product.

---

## Importance of Revenue Analysis

Revenue analysis can help businesses:

- Identify profitable products.
- Understand product performance.
- Compare sales contribution.
- Make better pricing decisions.
- Improve inventory planning.

# 🏪 Dashboard 3 – Store and Product Price Analysis

## Store-Wise Price Analysis

The **Sum of Price by Store** visualization compares the total prices of products available across different stores.

The stores included in the analysis are:

- Costco
- Target
- Walmart

This comparison helps users understand how product prices differ between different retail stores.

Store-wise analysis can be useful for identifying pricing patterns and comparing overall product values.

---

## Product and Store Comparison

The **Sum of Price by Product and Store** chart provides a detailed comparison of product prices across different stores.

The products included in the visualization include:

- Rice
- Dried Beans
- Bottled Water
- Milk
- Canned Vegetables

Each product is compared across multiple stores, making it easier to identify differences in pricing.

---

## Importance of Store Comparison

Store comparison helps businesses and customers understand:

- Price differences between stores.
- Which store has higher product prices.
- Which products have significant price variations.
- Overall pricing trends across retailers.

This type of analysis can support better business decisions and help customers compare product pricing.
